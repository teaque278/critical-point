About

Life isn’t about the big turning points and milestones. It’s about the tiny critical points. At 23, there have been uncountable people, art and situations that have shaped the way I think today. At 11, my former best friend introduced me to Taylor Swift. Critical point. At 13, I used Omegle for the first time. Critical point. At 16, I watched my first play and learnt how to appreciate live theatre. Critical point. At 19, I considered myself to know my way around good Kpop. Critical point. At 20, I learnt to crochet & perform Reiki. Critical point. At 23, I don’t know anything. A critical point for real. So this archive is to document all my critical points and growth through them.
