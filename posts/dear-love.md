dear love,  
you were blue since I opened my eyes into this world  
blue of a clear glass bottle, holding memories soaked in light.  
memories of tiny hands holding bigger ones,  
of laughter past bedtime,  
of wearing your interests as my own,  
of being your first child.  
you were blue since I was born,  
blue of a curacao lemonade in the sweltering summer heat. 


dear love,  
you were purple when i was 13,  
purple of the swirling patterns behind my eyes, always slightly out of reach. 
you were the tint of that sparkly eyeshadow that made me feel like the prettiest girl in the world. 
but you were also the shade of my fingers that strained trying to hold you together. 
you were purple when I was 13,  
purple of a venom made from mixing two complete opposites. 


dear love,  
you were red when i was 19,  
exactly as they said you would be one day. 
you were red of the string I believed tied us together,  
tangle, stretch, but never break. 
I had to cut it with my right hand, again and again till it frayed and strayed and finally broke. 
you were red when I was 19,  
red of a rose that withered inside a beautiful book. 


dear love,  
you’re yellow, now that i’m 23,  
the colour that only ever reached me in sunlit patches and sweet nothings. 
but you’re real, aren’t you?  
I want to hold your light without snuffing you out,  
I’ll be a rainbow so you don’t have to search for the right shade again.  
you’re yellow now that i’m 23,  
yellow of the sparks that fly when you smile.
