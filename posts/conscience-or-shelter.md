God is either your conscience or the shelter you seek when life goes to shit.

Nobody is born with reverence or fear towards God. If you believe in God, it’s most likely because that’s how you were raised. When a child tells lies, it is difficult to explain to them why lying is bad. Parents need time, patience and enough empathy to sit their child down and explain to them how their lies affect other people and why they should care about not hurting those around them. It is complex, having to explain why you shouldn’t hurt another person for your personal gain. You need to build a foundational idea of what is good or bad for it to make sense to a child.

And how do you create this foundational idea?

Tell kids about an omnipresent entity that can read their minds and punish them for bad actions.

Don’t steal because God is watching.

Never lie to your parents because God is watching.

If you don’t follow what is prescribed by God, he will punish you.

God is a way to develop foundational conscience in children. All of one’s religious ideologies and leanings are built on top of this foundational conscience, but that’s another tangent altogether.

What if you did not learn about God growing up? Can you still learn to believe in God as an adult?

100%

I say this as an atheist, faith saves lives.

Existentialist philosopher Albert Camus said that humans desire for purpose, order and meaning in this universe that’s silent & quite honestly, indifferent.

Everything we do in the pursuit of this meaning thus becomes our struggle to create meaning and faith–faith is what keeps some of us afloat.
